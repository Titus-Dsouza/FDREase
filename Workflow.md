# Workflow.md — AICTE FDR Portal

## Institution Workflow
1. Log in to the portal.
2. Enter FDR details and upload the document.
3. Review extracted details and correct mistakes.
4. Submit the application.
5. Track status and respond to correction requests.
6. View the final decision and maturity reminders.

## AI Processing
1. Extract text using OCR.
2. Validate required fields, dates, and amounts.
3. Flag possible duplicates and inconsistencies.
4. Use AI to explain potential issues.
5. Send results to the official for review.

## AICTE Official Workflow
1. Log in to the official dashboard.
2. View submitted applications and validation warnings.
3. Review the document and supporting details.
4. Approve, reject, or request corrections.
5. Record the decision and review notes.

## Application Statuses
- **Draft** — Not submitted.
- **Submitted** — Awaiting review.
- **Under Review** — Being checked.
- **Correction Required** — Institution must make changes.
- **Approved** — Officially approved.
- **Rejected** — Officially rejected.

## Important Rules
- AI assists; authorized officials make final decisions.
- AI warnings do not prove fraud.
- Bank verification must not be presented as real unless an authorized service confirms it.
- Clearly label simulated data and verification in the demo.
